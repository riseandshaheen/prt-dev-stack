# Sling at the production snapshot gap: quick bundle

Oct 6, 2026 · sling `ghcr.io/riseandshaheen/sling-node:3.0.0-alpha.5` at its default `--snapshot-gap-inputs 64`, reference node `test-contracts-bump`, echo app · run `harness/runs/20261006-003029/GAP64/` (`console.log` holds every check; the teardown files were not written because Docker failed after the run)

dave's e2e suite runs the sling at snapshot gap 2, and its own notes say batch GC, multi-input advance batches and dispute replay past a boundary never run at gap 1. The published image defaults to 64. This bundle checks the batch paths at 64 without a dispute.

## Result: no divergence

| Epoch | Inputs | What it exercises | Input range | Final state | Commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 63 | One short batch, flushed at seal | agree | agree | agree |
| 3 | 64 | Exactly one full batch | agree | agree | agree |
| 4 | 65 | One full batch plus one | agree | agree | agree |
| 5 | 129 | Two full batches plus one | agree | agree | agree |
| 6 | 100 | Sling restarted with one batch done and 36 inputs pending | agree | agree | agree |
| 7 | 66 | 62 inputs, then 4 more in the sealing block ahead of the accept, crossing the boundary at the seal | agree | agree | agree |

Every epoch was accepted on chain with the expected input count, and chain, sling and reference node agree on bounds, final state and commitment. The restart in epoch 6 happened at block 1639, two blocks after sling's last processed block, and sling recovered without error.

## Observations

**Sling's state directory grows fast at gap 64.** Measured after each epoch sealed:

| After epoch | Inputs | State dir |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 63 | 390 MB |
| 3 | 64 | 1,165 MB |
| 4 | 65 | 1,742 MB |
| 5 | 129 | 2,330 MB |
| 6 | 100 | 2,330 MB |

That is roughly 600 MB per epoch with inputs, levelling off at 2.3 GB, which suggests snapshot GC keeps a bounded window. This is an operational sizing note, not a bug; whether the plateau holds over many more epochs was not measured.

**dave's Lua oracle cannot replay this history on a normal disk.** It stores a full machine snapshot per input under `/tmp` and fails to delete them ("Error removing file … Directory not empty"). Replaying 321 inputs to reach epoch 5, it filled the host disk at input 88 of the last epoch, after 235 s, and crashed. That check failure says nothing about the nodes. It does change the cost of the full dispute test: a sybil at this size needs tens of GB of scratch space, or a patched cleanup. The harness now caps that scratch at 6 GB (`--tmpfs /tmp:size=6g`) and refuses to start a sybil or oracle with less than 20 GB free.

## Not covered

- A dispute inside a large epoch, where the defender replays up to 63 inputs per step. This is the path most likely to differ at gap 64, and it needs the sybil scratch problem solved first.
- Killing sling mid-dispute at gap 64.
- Epochs past a few hundred inputs, and snapshot growth over many epochs.
